\begin{itemize}
\item Joystick 1 (port 1) plays for player 1 and joystick 2 (port 2) for player 2, together with the keys.
\item The shot leaves when you \textbf{release} the fire button. You can keep walking while you hold it.
\item A turn is a quarter turn. Holding the turn key keeps turning, one quarter after another.
\item Player 2 turns by holding \key{SHIFT} and pressing \key{A} or \key{D} (they do not step then). \key{CTRL} + \key{A} / \key{D} fires while stepping sideways, like \key{SPACE} + \key{$\leftarrow$} / \key{$\rightarrow$} for player 1.
\item The \textbf{special attack} costs energy: the view flashes red, every robot in sight is stunned and hurt, and the robots' shots in sight disappear.
\item In the shop, up and down choose and fire buys (section 7). On the screens that say \texttt{PRESS FIRE}, fire goes on.
\end{itemize}
