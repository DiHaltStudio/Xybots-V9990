\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{DiHalt Studio logo}, 4 seconds without a Moonsound, or 10 seconds with its music and voices on a Moonsound. It cannot be skipped.
\item \textbf{The cover picture}: Rock and Ace with the \texttt{XYBOTS} title. It stays until you press \key{SPACE} or a joystick button. It is shown only once after switching on.
\item \textbf{The title screen}: the two views say \texttt{PRESS FIRE TO START}; above them the name of the game, the authors and \texttt{HI:} with the high score. With a Moonsound the arcade title music plays.
\item \textbf{The game}: player 1 starts with \key{SPACE} or the button of joystick 1, player 2 with \key{CTRL} or the button of joystick 2. Each player has a view of their own: Rock on the left, Ace on the right. Player 2 can join later as well (section 5).
\end{enumerate}
When no player is left in the game and the credits are used up, the game goes back to the title screen (section 8).
